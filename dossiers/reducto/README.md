# Target dossier: Reducto (document AI platform)

**What this is.** A living behavioral dossier on Reducto, built exclusively from Reducto's *public* materials — documentation, changelog, engineering blog, pricing pages, public GitHub artifacts, and a logged-in observation of its Studio/Office product surfaces. No proprietary code, no non-public information, no client material. This is the same competitive study every AI company performs on public surfaces; inferences are labeled as inferences.

**Why it lives here.** Timothy (Rayne's on-premises document parser) is being built to Reducto's functional standard without Reducto's cloud. This dossier is the reference the Timothy team builds against: what the product does, what it costs, how its builders say it works, and which of its published numbers can be trusted — with provenance attached to every figure.

**Provenance discipline (the house rule).** Every number in this dossier carries its class:
- **Vendor-stated** — Reducto's own claim, no public harness.
- **Commissioned** — third-party artifact paid for by Reducto (methodology may be disclosed; reproducibility may be limited).
- **Competitor-authored** — produced by a rival with its own ranking at stake.
- **Maintainer-reported** — benchmark maintainer's own run.
- **Independent** — no known interest.

Numbers without a class do not belong in this dossier. Errors found get a dated correction entry, not a silent edit.
